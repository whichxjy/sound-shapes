About the shapes

One note moves the line horizontally; the other moves it vertically. Their frequency ratio creates a Lissajous curve.

A mathematical picture, not the path of sound through air. Complexity doesn’t measure consonance.

Tuning & how it works

The collection is ordered by pitch. Each interval uses an illustrative just-intonation ratio above C4 (261.63 Hz), which may differ from the same notes on a modern piano.

Playback uses two pure sine tones. Visual phase changes only the drawing. What you hear also depends on timbre, register, tuning, and listening experience.
