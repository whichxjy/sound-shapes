FIELD NOTES03 / UNDERSTAND

A useful picture.
Not a verdict.

A Lissajous curve maps one oscillation to the horizontal axis and another to the vertical axis. Simple frequency ratios make paths that repeat quickly.

The curve is not the path sound takes through air. Its shape changes with phase, while the pitch interval remains the same. Consonance also depends on timbre, register, and listening experience.
