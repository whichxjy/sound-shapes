About the shapes

Two notes control horizontal and vertical movement. A third adds depth. Each axis represents one sine signal, and their frequency ratios create a repeating trajectory.

A mathematical picture, not the path of sound through air. Complexity doesn’t measure consonance.

Tuning & how it works

The interval collection is ordered by pitch. Intervals and chords use illustrative just-intonation ratios above C4 (261.63 Hz), which may differ from the same notes on a modern piano.

Pure uses sine tones; Soft adds gentle harmonics with a slow onset and quieter sustain. Curves show fundamental sine signals in either timbre. Visual phase changes only the drawing. What you hear also depends on timbre, register, tuning, and listening experience. The 3D view is an orthographic projection; camera rotation changes the outline without changing the signals. Pairwise views show the same chord’s signals; playback always includes all three notes.
